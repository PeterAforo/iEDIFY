INITIATOR AND LEAD STRATEGIST

Margaret Alexander Rehoboth

Margaret is an accomplished development practitioner and a visionary with over two decades of experience in furthering accountability to the interests of women/girls and other marginalized constituencies in development policy and action. She has worked to facilitate economic opportunities for women and contributed to increasing their representation, participation and actual involvement in leadership, governance and peace processes in society.

Dr. Cynthia Arku

Dr. Cynthia Arku is a socio-economic development researcher, corporate leadership and personal development consultant, lecturer in interdisciplinary studies, and mentor who is passionate to see that people live their full potential. Having trained in community planning, business development, and education, she has worked in community relations, business advancement and capacity building roles with the not-for-profit; municipal and provincial government; and the oil and gas sectors of Canada.

Dr Moses Deyegbe Kuvoame is an Associate Professor at the University of South-Eastern Norway (USN) and holds a PhD in Criminology and Sociology of Law from the Faculty of Law, University of Oslo. His research and professional work focus on youth marginalisation and empowerment, disability, mental health, substance misuse, crime and violence, and the design and evaluation of preventive and health-promoting interventions.

PROGRAM LEAD

Sandra Arthur

Sandra has over seventeen (17) years of hands-on experience in Development Work with emphasis on project development and management, financial management, capacity development, fund raising, facilitation and advocacy. She has successfully managed several donor funded projects, provided training to both individuals and Civil Society Organisations (CSOs) on good governance, transparency and accountability and development issues. Sandra has a keen interest in Social Accountability and promotion of social inclusion and has been facilitating Platforms to enhance service delivery and development for the ordinary citizens. She also has exposure in working with the public and private sectors and traditional authorities and media to establish the stage for engaging such key stakeholders in promoting policies and development at all levels.

RESEARCH & KNOWLEDGE MANAGEMENT LEAD

Afua Gyapomaa

Ms. Afua Gyapomaa is a seasoned Development Practitioner with over fifteen years of experience in programme design, advocacy, and social development. She has dedicated her career to promoting inclusive participation, equality, and empowerment among marginalized groups, particularly youth and women. Afua began her career at World Vision International—Ghana as a Programme Officer for Sponsorship, where she supported child-focused community development initiatives. She later joined ABANTU for Development, a leading women's rights organisation in Ghana, where she contributed to policy advocacy and action research aimed at enhancing women's representation and leadership in decision-making spaces.
