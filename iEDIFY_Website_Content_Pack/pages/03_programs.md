OUR PROGRAMS

Four Pillars of Impact

Strategic initiatives designed to address youth unemployment through advocacy, funding, education, and startup support across the African continent.

4

CORE PILLARS

5

COMMUNITY GROUPS

2

PARTNER TYPES

01
Advocacy, Awareness Raising and Sensitization

We engage with key stakeholders across the spectrum – including government agencies, policy/decision makers, political actors, diplomatic communities (particularly heads of African missions and other influential personalities in the international development arena), entrepreneurs/industry leaders, youth groups, civil society, opinion leaders, parents/guardian communities, educators and the media among others – to facilitate public discourse on the establishment of a revolving Trust Fund to support youth innovation and entrepreneurship in Africa.

POLICY DIALOGUE & PUBLIC AWARENESS

Stakeholder convenings on a revolving youth entrepreneurship trust fund
Public education on youth unemployment and enterprise solutions
Advocacy with governments, educators, and development partners
PILLAR 1
02
Establishment of a Sustainable Youth Innovation & Entrepreneurship Trust Fund

We aim to mobilize funds from international donors, private investors, and development partners to enable the creation of a revolving Fund that helps to provide vital seed capital for youth-led startups.

RESOURCE MOBILIZATION & CAPITAL ACCESS

Partnerships with donors, investors, and development finance actors
Design of a sustainable revolving fund structure
Pipeline development for youth-led enterprise investment
PILLAR 2
03
Integrate Innovation and Entrepreneurship into Education

We will partner with key educational stakeholders to mainstream entrepreneurship and innovation into secondary and postsecondary school curricula across the continent.

EDUCATION & SKILLS PATHWAYS

Entrepreneurship integration in secondary and post-secondary curricula
Training for school-to-work transitions
Innovation mindset development for young Africans
PILLAR 3
04
Launch a Seed Funding Scheme for Youth Startups

We will design and implement an accessible platform to provide young African innovators and entrepreneurs with the funding and support needed to turn their business concepts into reality.

STARTUP LAUNCH SUPPORT

Accessible application pathways for young founders
Mentorship and venture readiness support
Seed capital for promising youth-led business concepts
PILLAR 4
Our Operational Framework

Facilitate an integrated and a comprehensive synergistic ecosystem where policy enables capital, education builds capacity and regional hubs deliver localized impact at scale. Implementation to center around four core program pillars, namely:

Policy & Trust Fund Advocacy

Drive institutional sustainability through strategic policy engagement and advocacy.

Capitalization & Fund Management

Establish independent robust governance and financial architecture that ensure transparency, accountability, and sustainable capital deployment.

Education & Curriculum Integration

Work in partnership with educational stakeholders and industry leaders across the spectrum to build entrepreneurial capacity through training and practical skills development tailored to African market realities.

Seed Funding & Regional Hubs

Provide localized support through regional innovation hubs offering mentorship, networking, market access, and direct seed capital to high-potential ventures.

Associates

Individuals and businesses that have gone through our program successfully and have benefited from the iEDIFY Africa funding scheme.
