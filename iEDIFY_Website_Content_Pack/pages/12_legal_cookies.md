Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how International Entrepreneurial Development and Innovation Fund for Youth in Africa("IEDIFY africa") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website at https://iedifyafrica.org.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how you use the site.

Types of Cookies We Use
Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core features such as member authentication, admin access, and support chat sessions. Our authentication system uses a secure HTTP-only session cookie stored locally on our server — we do not use third-party authentication providers. You cannot opt out of essential cookies while using authenticated features.

Functional Cookies

These cookies allow us to remember choices you make (such as chat session state) and provide enhanced, personalized features during your visit.

What We Do Not Use

We do not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. We do not sell your data to advertisers. If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this policy and request consent where required.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may prevent you from signing in or using the support chat feature.

Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at info@iedifyafrica.org.
