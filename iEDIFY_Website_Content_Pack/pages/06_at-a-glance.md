AT A GLANCE

iEDIFY Africa
Theory of Change

A Strategic Imperative — Addressing youth unemployment through a comprehensive, multi-pronged approach.

The Challenge

Africa's 1.5+ billion population is the youngest globally, with over 60% under age 30. By 2030, 375 million youth risk underemployment or unemployment.

The Cost of Inaction
Economic stagnation, mounting debt burden & dependency
Irregular migration & human trafficking
Regional instability
Lost GDP growth potential among others
The Opportunity

AfCFTA presents the largest emerging trade bloc globally — over fifty countries, creating a generational opportunity for youth-led enterprise across borders.

54 Countries
1.5B People
$3.4T GDP
The Solution

A $100 million Youth Innovation & Entrepreneurship Trust Fund — a sustainable, revolving financing and capacity-building mechanism

HOW WE WORK

Operational Framework

Facilitate an integrated and a comprehensive synergistic ecosystem where policy enables capital, education builds capacity and regional hubs deliver localized impact at scale. Implementation to center around four core program pillars, namely:

Innovation

Fostering creative solutions for youth entrepreneurship and sustainable development.

Sustainability

Building long-term, self-sustaining programs that outlast initial funding cycles.

Education

Integrating innovation and entrepreneurship into formal and informal education systems.

Inclusion

Ensuring youth participation across gender, geography, and socioeconomic status.

Policy & Trust Fund Advocacy

Drive institutional sustainability through strategic policy engagement and advocacy.

Capitalization & Fund Management

Establish independent robust governance and financial architecture that ensure transparency, accountability, and sustainable capital deployment.

Education & Curriculum Integration

Work in partnership with educational stakeholders and industry leaders across the spectrum to build entrepreneurial capacity through training and practical skills development tailored to African market realities.

Seed Funding & Regional Hubs

Provide localized support through regional innovation hubs offering mentorship, networking, market access, and direct seed capital to high-potential ventures.
