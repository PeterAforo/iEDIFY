# Sandra Arthur

Source: https://www.iedifyafrica.org/team (expanded profile)

PROGRAM LEAD

Sandra Arthur

Sandra has over seventeen (17) years of hands-on experience in Development Work with emphasis on project development and management, financial management, capacity development, fund raising, facilitation and advocacy. She has successfully managed several donor funded projects, provided training to both individuals and Civil Society Organisations (CSOs) on good governance, transparency and accountability and development issues. Sandra has a keen interest in Social Accountability and promotion of social inclusion and has been facilitating Platforms to enhance service delivery and development for the ordinary citizens. She also has exposure in working with the public and private sectors and traditional authorities and media to establish the stage for engaging such key stakeholders in promoting policies and development at all levels.

Sandra also has over ten (10) years of experience in private sector work in the areas of marketing, administration and operations management. She has served in many capacities as marketing, administration and operations manager effectively. She as well functions well in customer experience roles through effective communication and inter-personal skills. She is passionate about the youth and underprivileged empowerment and progress as she believes in the fact that no matter the background, everybody can reach his or her potentials and make impact given the opportunity.

Sandra holds MBA in Project Management, Bsc in Business Administration, Fdip from Chartered Institute of Administrators and Management Consultants and HND Marketing. She also holds certificates in other Professional Development trainings at both local and international levels.

Some of projects and programs implemented are funded by the following donors: UNDP, GIZ, USAID, DFID, STAR-Ghana, OSIWA, Commonwealth Business Council, CFLI, UNODC, The World Bank.
