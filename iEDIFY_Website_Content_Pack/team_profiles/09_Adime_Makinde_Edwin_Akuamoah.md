# Adime Makinde Edwin-Akuamoah

Source: https://www.iedifyafrica.org/team (expanded profile)

Adime Makinde Edwin-Akuamoah

Adime Makinde Edwin-Akuamoah is a 22 years old Level 3 student of the Cape Coast Technical University. He is enrolled in an undergraduate program in Construction Technology and Management. His interest in the iEDIFY Africa initiative is informed by the entrepreneurial prospects it offers the youth of Africa.

He sees the program as providing him an opportunity to be exposed to trends in technology and the digital economy as it pertains to the construction sector. He is particularly interested the market and feasibility studies and innovation aspects of his studies. He also likes to learn about the history, evolution and strategies of construction companies.

He views business and entrepreneurship as blending creativity and functionality for the purpose of serving humanity.
