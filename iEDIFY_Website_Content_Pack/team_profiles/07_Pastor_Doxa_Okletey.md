# Pastor Doxa Okletey

Source: https://www.iedifyafrica.org/team (expanded profile)

ADVISORY BOARD MEMBER

Pastor Doxa Okletey

Pastor Doxa Okletey holds an MSc Water Science and Engineering from UNESCO-IHE, Delft, the Netherlands (2005), and BSc Geology from the University of Ghana, Legon, Accra, Ghana (1997). He also received a prestigious Ghana National Award – Grand Medal for Gallantry Civil Division (2000) for rescuing a colleague from an attack by a swarm of bees during a field work.

Pastor Doxa Okletey worked as a geologist and hydrogeologist for a decade especially with non-governmental organization (NGOs) in northern Ghana. He is also an ordained minister of the gospel who has been involved in youth activities including camp meetings, youth empowerment programs in churches in Accra, Takoradi and Aflao since 2009. He has a passion for youth transformation and loves to see the youth impacted with tangible evidence. He is currently enrolled in a doctoral program in Geology at the University of Ghana.
