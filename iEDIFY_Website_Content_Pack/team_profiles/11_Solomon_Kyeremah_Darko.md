# Solomon Kyeremah Darko

Source: https://www.iedifyafrica.org/team (expanded profile)

EDUCATION & TRAINING LEAD

Solomon Kyeremah Darko

Solomon Kyeremah-Darko is an education and training specialist with over a decade of experience in curriculum development, consultancy, and capacity building across Africa.

He has worked with Right To Play, ActionAid, Oxfam, and the International Trade Centre (ITC), designing innovative programs in play-based learning, campaign strategy, leadership, and youth development.

He is also an entrepreneur and founder of Okyeremah Crafts, an African fashion brand producing bespoke and ready-to-wear clothing for local and international markets.

He holds a Master's degree in Education, Training, and Development, and a Bachelor of Science in Agriculture.
