# Baffour Asare Amankwah

Source: https://www.iedifyafrica.org/team (expanded profile)

MONITORING & EVALUATION LEAD

Baffour Asare Amankwah

Baffour Asare Amankwa is a policy analyst and advocate with specialization in sustainable energy and environmental governance. He is keen to work closely with relevant stakeholders across the spectrum to design and facilitate ecosystems that promote youth innovation and entrepreneurship that is environmentally friendly and sustainable within the context of Africa.

He has over ten years of work experience in policy analysis & advocacy, stakeholder engagement, program design, monitoring and evaluation among others. He has worked with a range of public institutions, including the Kofi Annan International Peacekeeping Training Centre (KAIPTC) and the Ghana National Petroleum Corporation (GNPC), where he currently serves as the Senior Institutional Reporting Officer.

As a public servant, his passion is to contribute to policy and action that help to enhance the leadership skills of the youth while promoting their innovation and entrepreneurial prospects within the Africa region.

Baffour Asare Amankwah's educational background includes a Master's Degree in Gender, Peace and Security and a Bachelor's Degree in Geography & Rural Development. He has also completed a certificate program in Monitoring and Evaluation and is currently enrolled in a professional diploma program in International Environmental Management.
