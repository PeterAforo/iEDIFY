# Mr. Euclid Shilaku

Source: https://www.iedifyafrica.org/team (expanded profile)

ADVISORY BOARD MEMBER

Mr. Euclid Shilaku

As a UX strategist and digital innovation leader with over eight years of experience, Euclid is passionate about designing solutions that empower communities and drive inclusive growth. Currently spearheading Digital Innovation and AI Chat Capabilities at Ecobank, Euclid combines expertise in user-centered design with a deep commitment to leveraging technology for social impact.

Euclid brings to iEDIFY Africa's board a proven track record of transforming complex challenges into intuitive digital experiences, particularly in the financial sector. Euclid's work aligns seamlessly with iEDIFY's mission to unlock Africa's youth potential through entrepreneurship and innovation. By bridging the gap between user needs, technology, and business strategy, Euclid supports the organization's vision to equip young entrepreneurs with the tools and mindset needed to thrive in a digital economy.

Outside of work, Euclid actively mentors early-career designers and advocates for youth-focused initiatives across Africa.
