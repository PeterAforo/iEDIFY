# Monique Ohenewaa Nkrumah

Source: https://www.iedifyafrica.org/team (expanded profile)

ADVISORY BOARD MEMBER

Monique Ohenewaa Nkrumah

Monique Ohenewaa Nkrumah is a lawyer, humanitarian advocate, youth leader, and the founder of the She Rise Network, an initiative focused on inspiring and empowering young girls to build confidence, discover their potential, and become leaders in their communities.

She holds an LLB Degree from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST) and completed her professional legal training at the Ghana School of Law in 2022. She also successfully participated in a specialized training in humanitarian response at Harvard University. Her work and professional interests lie at the intersection of law, human rights, humanitarian action, youth development, and international affairs.

Monique has participated in various youth-focused programs and professional forums, where she moderated and facilitated, engaging young people on leadership, confidence building, discovering their purpose, and personal development. She is particularly interested in international cooperation and diplomacy and is committed to contributing to initiatives that promote inclusive leadership, meaningful opportunities, and positive change for young Africans.

She looks forward to bringing her experience in law, humanitarian advocacy and youth development to bear on the work of iEDIFY Africa, as she supports the various initiatives that help to create meaningful opportunities for the continent's youth.
